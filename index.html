Welcome to The Hero’s Notebook

The Hero’s Notebook is a fan website that covers a lot of the many things that is included in The Legend of Zelda, a popular game franchise made by Nintendo. On this page, you’ll be able to click any of the sub-headings above and be brought to that page that explains more. With this website, I hope that you’ll be able to learn more about the Legend of Zelda Franchise and also become a fan of what it has to offer.
