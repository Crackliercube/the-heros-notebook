For as long as I can remember, I have been playing Zelda games all my life and get them as soon as they come out. My first game was Ocarina of Time on my 3DS and it changed my perspective on games forever. Since that, I have collected almost every physical game and have played them all through in their full glory.

Project Overview:

For this project, I wanted to use the knowledge I have on The Legend of Zelda and put all my thoughts and knowledge into a website.
